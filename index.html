About

Beyond the lab

My work centers on how people interact with systems: digital interfaces, wearable sensors, and how physical spaces are organized. I'm currently a Robotics Data Operations Expert at Meta, a research assistant in UW's Human and Systems Lab, and president of UW's chapter of the Human Factors and Ergonomics Society.

Outside of engineering, I've played violin in solo and ensemble settings for 20 years. At UW I've performed with chamber groups, the UW Symphony Orchestra, Campus Philharmonia Orchestra, and the student organization Music for Charity. You can see some of my performances here!

I also foster dogs, cook and bake, and play a lot of board games.
