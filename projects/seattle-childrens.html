Internship · People Strategy

Project Management Toolkit at Seattle Children's Hospital

Duration
Jun – Aug 2025
Team
People Strategy, People & Culture division
Methods
Informational interviews · Template design · User testing

The goal

Give leaders across the People and Culture division a way to get familiar with project management tools and use them on their own projects.

What I did

I conducted informational interviews to understand the project management challenges people had run into in their work, consulted with the People Strategy team on which tools would best support those needs, and then created and user tested beginner-friendly templates of the tools they recommended.

I also supported and attended journey mapping and service improvement workshops focused on improving the employee experience at the hospital.

Outcome

A toolkit of project management resources for projects across the People and Culture division, designed so leaders in other areas could pick up and use project management tools with confidence.
